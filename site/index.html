logging4s

Transforming raw data into structured logs – like beavers building a dam!

Quick Start

Get up and running with logging4s in just a few minutes.

      
        libraryDependencies += "org.logging4s" %% "logging4s-core" % "latest-version"

Why logging4s?

Just like beavers efficiently process wood into strong structures, logging4s processes data into structured logs.

Seamlessly integrates with Cats Effect and ZIO, supporting major JSON libraries like Circe, Jsoniter, Json4s, and uPickle.

Effortless JSON logging makes it simple to send logs to Kibana and other analytics platforms.
